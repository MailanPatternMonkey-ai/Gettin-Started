1. The hard blocker: odd winding is absent from the fully summed periodic dual ensemble

This is the most important thing I found.

In the exact periodic dual partition function, define the mod-two current chain

\[
\bar I_\ell=I_\ell\pmod 2.
\]

The link constraint is

\[
I_\ell+M_\ell\equiv0\pmod2,
\qquad
M_\ell=\sum_{p\supset\ell}M_p.
\]

But \(M_\ell\) is precisely the mod-two boundary of the plaquette 2-chain \(M\). Therefore

\[
\boxed{\bar I=\partial M.}
\]

A boundary is homologically trivial. Consequently, on the three-torus,

\[
[\bar I]=0\in H_1(T^3,\mathbb Z_2),
\]

and the mod-two current flux through every noncontractible cut obeys

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal W_\alpha^{(2)}
=
\sum_{\ell\perp\Sigma_\alpha}I_\ell
\equiv0\pmod2.
}
\]

This is not merely a \(t=0\) property.

It holds at every \(t\) in the fully link-summed periodic ensemble represented by the written dual formula. Charge-six sources do not change it because

\[
\nabla\!\cdot I=-6n
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\nabla\!\cdot I\equiv0\pmod2.
\]

CKT’s published dual formula imposes this same link-parity constraint, but their supplement then permits odd-current updates only along one axis after imposing \(u_{ij}=1\) on all links in that direction. That axial condition changes the finite-volume topological-sector construction: their odd sector is not automatically the unconstrained observable of Eq. 12 as written. 

What that means

The older claim

> “The dual representation makes deconfinement a manifestly gauge-invariant parity statement needing no gauge fixing or sector choice”



is not correct as written.

It does not mean CKT’s physical conclusion is necessarily wrong. It means the protocol must specify exactly which finite-volume ensemble their odd-current statistic belongs to.

A clean formulation is to retain a background holonomy \(\eta_z=\pm1\) instead of summing it away. Let

\[
Z_+=Z_{\rm even}+Z_{\rm odd},
\]

while an antiperiodic seam or negative background holonomy weights odd winding by a minus sign,

\[
Z_-=Z_{\rm even}-Z_{\rm odd}.
\]

Then, provided the seam construction is derived exactly,

\[
\boxed{
f_{\rm odd}
=
\frac{Z_{\rm odd}}{Z_++0}
=
\frac12\left(1-\frac{Z_-}{Z_+}\right).
}
\]

More simply,

\[
f_{\rm odd}=\frac{Z_{\rm odd}}{Z_{\rm even}+Z_{\rm odd}}.
\]

The fully holonomy-summed gauge partition function gives

\[
Z_++Z_-=2Z_{\rm even},
\]

which explains why odd topological winding disappears after all global holonomies are summed.

Mandatory Q2 repair

Appendix A must now define one of these exactly:

1. a maximal-tree or axial gauge with explicit residual holonomy variables;


2. a fixed-holonomy current ensemble;


3. a seam-modified dual ensemble;


4. periodic and antiperiodic partition functions whose ratio reconstructs the odd-sector weight.



It must then prove:

the direct and dual ensembles are identical;

which links retain or lose parity projectors;

how global holonomies are counted;

how the CKT axial implementation maps onto the chosen ensemble;

whether \(f_{\rm odd}\) is a sector-conditioned statistic or a ratio of physical partition functions;

detailed balance and sector reachability;

agreement with exact or high-precision small-volume enumeration.


Until that is complete:

\[
\boxed{\text{Q2 GATE CLOSED}.}
\]
