1. Microscopic Lattice Hamiltonian
Let \Lambda = L^3 be a 3D simple cubic lattice with periodic boundary conditions.
 * Matter degrees of freedom: Onsite complex rotor phases \theta_i \in [0, 2\pi) defined on sites i \in \Lambda, where z_i = e^{i\theta_i}.
 * Gauge degrees of freedom: Discrete link variables \sigma_{ij} = \sigma_{ji} \in \{+1, -1\} defined on directed nearest-neighbor edges \langle ij \rangle.
 * Gauge transformations: \theta_i \to \theta_i + \pi s_i and \sigma_{ij} \to s_i \sigma_{ij} s_j, where s_i \in \{+1, -1\}.
The frozen lattice action is:

 * K \ge 0: Matter hopping parameter.

 * h_6 \ge 0: Explicit sixfold anisotropy coupling (enforcing the leading allowed onsite operator).
 * Physical composite order parameter: \Phi_i = z_i^2 = e^{i 2\theta_i}.

Update Cycle
