Exact mathematical repair wording
These supporting amendments adapt the September 6 review. They follow the foundational preservation clause on page 1 and do not narrow §10. The predecessor remains intact. [S5; S7]
A. §0 — shared membership and reciprocal contact
Target: replace the “Equivalently” paragraph and its membership display with the following passage. Retain the surrounding non-sovereign-field constraints.
Both endpoints belong to the declared shared field: O_D ∈ ℒ and S_D ∈ ℒ. Reciprocal contact, written O_D ↔_ℒ S_D, is a separate relation that must be specified or observed in the application. Common membership alone does not establish that relation. The field’s non-sovereign role is a declared constitutional constraint. The three-term representation is a declared architecture; the notation alone supplies neither a topology nor a physical or platform field.
Reason: Two distinct points can belong to the same set while the contact relation is empty. Membership therefore cannot establish reciprocal contact. This repairs one implication without introducing a new field theory.
B. §5 — invariant domain and complete quantification
Target: replace the theorem setup through “That is the closure earned by descent.” Keep the following closure-status block, subject to the clarified domain below.
Fix a declared domain E and a deterministic update U with U(E) ⊆ E. Define R(x) = ρ(x,Ux) = (ΔC, ΔD, ΔK) for x ∈ E, using the declared typed difference rules. Put x ∼_R y exactly when R(x) = R(y); the residual carrier is R(E), identified with E/∼_R.
There is a unique function F: R(E) → R(E) satisfying F(R(x)) = R(Ux) for every x ∈ E if and only if, for all x,y ∈ E, R(x) = R(y) implies R(Ux) = R(Uy). Eligibility restrictions must be incorporated into the declared domain and its update rule. Checks on selected pairs establish closure only when their coverage or an accompanying argument proves this condition for every same-residual class in E.
A located same-residual/different-successor pair in E establishes FAILED for the proposed deterministic factor. An untested, unknown, underspecified, or undercovered test remains UNRESOLVED. A finite trace needs an explicit treatment of its final successor before it can serve as an invariant domain; no terminal self-loop may be invented to complete the record.
Proof: If F exists, equal inputs have equal outputs. Conversely, the implication makes F(R(x)) = R(Ux) independent of the representative. Forward invariance keeps outputs in R(E), and every element of R(E) has a representative, giving uniqueness.
C. §9 — replace checklist item 5
