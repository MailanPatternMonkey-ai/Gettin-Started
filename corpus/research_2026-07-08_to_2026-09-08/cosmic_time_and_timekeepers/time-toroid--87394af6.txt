Now put actual electromagnetic character underneath those nodes.

Uranus: extremely asymmetric EM geometry: magnetic axis tilted about 59–60°, dipole offset by roughly one-third of a planetary radius, with a magnetotail twisted into a corkscrew by its rotation. (NASA)

Neptune: magnetic axis tilted about 47°, with a highly variable magnetosphere; NASA gives its field strength as about 27 times Earth's. (NASA Science)

Pluto: intrinsic global magnetic field is not established; what is measured is a structured interaction between its escaping atmosphere and the heliospheric solar wind, including a long heavy-ion tail. (NASA)

Moon: no global magnetic field, but localized crustal fields interact with and locally deflect the solar wind. (NASA Science)
